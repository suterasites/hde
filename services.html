01 / CCTV

CCTV Drain Inspections & Reports

When a drain isn't running right, we put a camera down the line and find out exactly why, without digging up the yard to guess. You get a clear rundown of what we found and what it takes to fix it, and where you need it, a written report with photos and footage for your records, a sale or a builder.

  • In-pipe camera survey of sewer, stormwater and sink drains
  • Blockage, crack, root-intrusion and collapse detection
  • Pipe locating and depth marking above ground
  • Written report with photos and footage on request
  • Pre-purchase and pre-handover inspections

02 / Blocked drains

Blocked Drains & Jetting

Blocked toilet, gurgling drain, or water backing up in the yard? We clear blocked sewer, stormwater and sink drains fast with high-pressure water jetting, which cuts through grease, roots and debris that a plunger or snake won't shift. Then we run a camera through to make sure it's properly clear and show you what caused it, so it doesn't come straight back.

  • High-pressure water jetting
  • Root cutting and grease removal
  • Sewer, stormwater and sink drains
  • Camera check after clearing
  • Same-day service where possible

03 / Non-destructive digging

Non-Destructive Digging (Vac Truck)

When you need to dig near live gas, water, power or comms, our vacuum excavation truck does it without the risk of a strike. It sucks the soil out cleanly instead of cutting through it, so the services stay intact and the site stays tidy. Ideal for potholing, pit and pier-hole cleaning, and getting into tight spots a machine can't reach.

  • Safe digging around live services
  • Potholing to locate and expose services
  • Pit, pier-hole and trench cleaning
  • Access for tight or sensitive sites
  • Clean, low-disturbance excavation
