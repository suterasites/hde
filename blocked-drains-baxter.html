What we do

Baxter blockages, cleared properly

Frequently asked

Baxter blocked drain questions.

Can you clear a blocked drain in Baxter today?

Wherever we can, yes. We're only a short drive from Baxter in Somerville, so call early and we'll do our best to clear your blockage the same day, then run a camera through to confirm it's clear.

What causes blocked drains in Baxter homes?

In Baxter it's often roots and debris building up in the long drain runs that come with larger, semi-rural blocks. We clear it with high-pressure jetting and camera the line to see whether it was a one-off or a bigger issue like root intrusion into a cracked pipe.

Will the blockage come back?

That depends on the cause. After we clear the line we run a camera through to check. If it's structural, like root intrusion or a broken pipe, we'll show you and talk through a lasting fix so you're not clearing the same Baxter drain every few months.

What drains can you clear?

Sewer, stormwater and sink drains at homes, units and commercial sites across Baxter. Blocked toilets, gurgling drains, water backing up in the yard and slow sinks and showers, we clear the lot.
