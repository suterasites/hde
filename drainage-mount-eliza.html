Frequently asked

Mount Eliza drainage questions.

Do you cover Mount Eliza?

Yes. Mount Eliza is close to our Somerville base and one of our regular areas. We look after drainage on established homes as well as renovation and new-build works.

My Mount Eliza drain keeps blocking with tree roots. Can you fix it?

Yes. Established blocks in Mount Eliza often have mature trees and older pipes, which is a common cause of recurring blockages. We clear it with high-pressure jetting and run a camera through to see whether it's a one-off or root intrusion into a cracked pipe, then talk you through a lasting fix.

Can you dig without wrecking established gardens and landscaping?

Yes. Our vac truck does non-destructive digging, using suction rather than a machine bucket, so we can pothole and excavate around services and mature landscaping with minimal disturbance and a tidy finish.

Do you do CCTV inspections in Mount Eliza before a renovation or purchase?

Yes. A CCTV inspection shows the true condition of the drains before you commit to a purchase or a renovation, without digging. You get a clear picture and a written report with footage where you need it.
