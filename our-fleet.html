Plant & vehicles

Everything we need, on the truck.

We run and maintain our own gear, so the right machine shows up for the job and it's ready to go. Everything is regularly serviced and checked to keep sites safe, efficient and on program.

  • 0.7t to 8t

    Excavators

    Seven excavators across the sizes: a 0.7-tonne for tight access, plus 1.7t, 2.5t, two 5.5t and two 8-tonne machines. We bring the right size to the site instead of forcing the job with the wrong one.

  • Jets to 225mm

    CCTV & jetting truck

    Runs a camera through the drain to find the fault, then clears it with high-pressure water. It jets pipe up to 225mm in diameter, so most blockages are sorted in the one visit.

  • 4500L vac tank

    Non-destructive digging truck

    A dedicated NDD vacuum truck with a 4500-litre tank, for digging safely around live gas, water, power and comms. No strikes, no damage, where a machine can't go.

  • Safe deep excavation

    Deep sewer shoring

    Shoring gear to support the sides of deep trenches, so we can work safely at depth on sewer and drainage that sits well below the surface.

  • Spoil & materials

    Trucks & trailers

    Tip trucks, trailers and heavy vehicles to cart spoil off site and get materials on, so the dig keeps moving and the site stays clean and clear.

  • Site support

    Work utes

    Fully kitted work utes to keep the crew, tools and fuel moving across the Peninsula and South East Melbourne, so the job never waits on gear.

Why it matters

Owned, run and maintained by us.

Running our own fleet means we control the quality, the timing and the safety, on every job.

  • 01

    Owner-operated

    You deal with the people who run the gear, not a subcontractor chain. That means one contact, one standard, and no surprises about who's turning up.

  • 02

    Serviced and maintained

    Everything is regularly serviced and checked, so it's safe and ready when it turns up. Reliable gear keeps your job on program instead of waiting on breakdowns.

  • 03

    The right machine for the job

    A range of sizes means we match the machine to the access and the dig, not the other way around. Less damage to the site, and the job done properly.
