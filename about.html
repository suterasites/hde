About us

Local drainage and excavation, done properly since 2009.

We're a family-run, VBA-licensed drainage and civil contractor based in Somerville, working across the Mornington Peninsula and South East Melbourne. Here's who we are and how we work.

Our story

Family-run, out of Somerville, since 2009.

Hoad Drainage & Excavations is a family-run drainage and civil contractor based in Somerville on the Mornington Peninsula. Jarryd has been a licensed plumbing practitioner since 2009, and we've been on the tools across the Peninsula and South East Melbourne ever since.

We do the jobs people call us for most, CCTV inspections, blocked drains and jetting, and non-destructive digging, alongside full sewer, stormwater and civil drainage for homeowners, builders and developers.

Local and equipped

Set up for the job, close to home.

We're based on the Peninsula and run our own gear, including a vacuum excavation truck for digging safely around live services. Being local means we can get to a blockage quickly and we know the ground we're working on, from Peninsula new builds through to the eastern suburbs.

Whether it's a single blocked drain or the underground stage of a build, you deal with the same team from the first call to the finished job.

How we work

Straightforward, and easy to deal with.

The same few things, on every job, whether it's a house call or a commercial site.

  • 01

    Licensed and compliant

    We're VBA-licensed for sewer and stormwater drainage. We notify the VBA and issue a certificate of compliance on completion, so it's on record for your inspector and your files.

  • 02

    We turn up and keep you posted

    We treat your program as ours. We turn up when we say we will, and we'll tell you early if something in the plans is going to cause a problem later, not once it's a hole in the ground.

  • 03

    One contact for the whole job

    Drainage, excavation and civil under one roof. One quote, one crew and one set of paperwork, from the camera down the line to the dig and the backfill.
