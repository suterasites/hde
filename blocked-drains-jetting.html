Signs you've got a blockage

When to get us out

A blockage rarely fixes itself, and the longer it sits the messier it gets. Call us if you're seeing any of these.

  • Toilet that won't flush or backs up
  • Gurgling drains or slow-draining sinks and showers
  • Water pooling in the yard or around a gully
  • Bad smells coming from a drain
  • A drain that keeps blocking no matter what you do

Frequently asked

Blocked drain questions.

Do you clear blocked drains the same day?

Wherever we can, yes. Call us early and we'll do our best to get to a blockage the same day. We clear it with high-pressure jetting, then run a camera through to confirm it's clear and show what caused it.

What is high-pressure jetting?

Jetting uses a high-pressure water hose to cut through grease, tree roots and debris and flush the line clean. It clears blockages a plunger or a snake won't shift, and it scours the pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the blockage.

Will the blockage just come back?

That depends on what caused it. After we clear the line we run a camera through to see whether it was a one-off or a bigger problem like root intrusion or a broken pipe. If it's structural we'll show you and talk through a lasting fix, so you're not paying to clear the same drain every few months.

What drains can you clear?

Sewer, stormwater and sink drains at homes, units and commercial sites. Blocked toilets, gurgling drains, water backing up in the yard, slow-draining sinks and showers, we clear the lot.
