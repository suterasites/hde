Frequently asked

Somerville civil & commercial questions.

Do you work with builders and developers in Somerville?

Yes, that's a big part of what we do. We handle the underground drainage stage for builders and developers on domestic, unit and commercial sites across Somerville, working to your program with one point of contact so it doesn't hold up the trades behind us.

Are you actually based in Somerville?

Yes. Our yard and office are at 5 Arduina Street, Somerville, and that is where the excavators, the vac truck and the jetting truck are kept. A Somerville site is the shortest run we do, which is why we can usually get someone back out the same day if the job needs a second look.

Can you handle unit-site and multi-dwelling drainage in Somerville?

Yes. Unit developments and multi-dwelling sites are core work for us, from the setout through to connection, with the compliance and documentation sorted along the way. Somerville and the surrounding Peninsula townships are where most of that work sits.

Do you do the excavation as well as the drainage?

Yes. Excavation, non-destructive digging, drainage and cut and seal all come under the one contact, so the underground stage of your Somerville site is handled end to end rather than split across trades.
