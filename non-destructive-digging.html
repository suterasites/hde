When you need to dig near live gas, water, power or comms, our vacuum excavation truck does it without the risk of a strike. It sucks the soil out cleanly instead of cutting through it, so the services stay intact and the site stays tidy, across the Mornington Peninsula and South East Melbourne.

What we do

Dig near live services without the risk

Our vac truck breaks up the ground with water or air and vacuums the soil straight out, instead of a machine bucket cutting through whatever's down there. That means we can pothole, clean out pits and pier holes, and get into tight spots a machine can't reach, all without damaging the services around them.

  • Safe digging around live gas, water, power and comms

How it works

Careful work by design. Here's how a vac truck job runs.

1

Locate services

We identify what's underground first, so we know exactly what we're working around before a thing is disturbed.

2

Frequently asked

Vac truck questions.

What is non-destructive digging?

Non-destructive digging, or vacuum excavation, uses high-pressure water or air to break up the soil and a powerful vacuum to suck it out, instead of cutting through the ground with a machine bucket. It exposes what's underground without damaging it, which makes it the safe way to dig around live services.

Why use a vac truck instead of an excavator?

Around live gas, water, power and comms, a machine bucket risks a strike, which is dangerous and expensive. A vac truck removes the soil cleanly and leaves the services intact, so it's the right call for potholing, working near assets and getting into tight or sensitive spots a machine can't.

What do you use the vac truck for?

Potholing to locate and expose services before you dig, cleaning out pits, pier holes and trenches, and excavating in tight access or sensitive sites. It's clean, low-disturbance and leaves the site tidy.

Can you work in tight access?

Yes. The suction hose reaches spots a machine can't get to, so we can excavate in tight, awkward or sensitive locations without tearing up everything around them.
