Frequently asked

Mornington drainage questions.

Do you cover Mornington?

Yes. Mornington is right in our backyard from our Somerville base, so it's one of our core areas for both home drainage and the underground stage of renovations and new builds.

Can you handle the drainage for a Mornington renovation or new build?

Yes. We do a lot of renovation and new-build drainage in Mornington, from sewer and stormwater connections to a full new-build layout. We're VBA-notified and issue a Certificate of Compliance on completion, so it's sorted for your builder, your inspector and your records.

Do you do CCTV inspections in Mornington before I buy or renovate?

Yes. A camera down the line before you buy or start a renovation shows exactly what condition the drains are in, without digging. You get a clear rundown and, where you need it, a written report with photos and footage.

Can you dig safely around services on an established Mornington block?

Yes. Our vac truck does non-destructive digging, so we can pothole and excavate around live gas, water, power and comms without the risk of a strike, and without tearing up established gardens and landscaping.
