What we do

See inside your Cranbourne drain before you dig

We put a camera down the line and find out exactly why a Cranbourne drain is slow, blocked or backing up, without tearing up the yard to guess. Cranbourne's reactive clay soils shift with the seasons and crack rigid pipe, which is often the real cause of a drain that keeps blocking. You get a straight rundown of what we found and what it takes to fix it, and where you need it, a written report with photos and footage for your records, a sale or a builder. It's the same CCTV drain inspection service we run right across the Peninsula, here in Cranbourne.

  • In-pipe camera survey of sewer, stormwater and sink drains
  • Blockage, tree-root, crack and collapse detection
  • Pipe locating and depth marking above ground
  • Written report with photos and footage on request
  • Pre-purchase and pre-handover inspections

Frequently asked

Cranbourne CCTV inspection questions.

Do you do CCTV drain inspections in Cranbourne?

Yes. We're based just up the road in Somerville and CCTV inspections in Cranbourne and Clyde are a regular job for us. We feed a camera through the line and show you exactly what's going on, without digging.

Can you inspect the drains before I buy a Cranbourne property?

Yes. Pre-purchase CCTV inspections are common in Cranbourne, where reactive clay ground is hard on rigid pipe, so cracked and misaligned joints are common, especially on older estates. We give you a clear picture of the drain's condition and, where you need it, a written report with photos and footage for the sale.

Do I get a report with the inspection?

Where you need one, yes. We can supply a written report with photos and footage for a sale, a builder or your own records. Just let us know when you book.

Do you need to dig anything up for the camera?

No. We access the drain through an existing point such as an inspection opening, gully or pit, so there's no digging to find the fault.
