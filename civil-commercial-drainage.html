Frequently asked

Civil & commercial questions.

Do you work with builders and developers?

Yes, that's a big part of what we do. We handle the underground drainage stage for builders and developers on domestic, unit and commercial sites, working to your program with one point of contact so it doesn't hold up the trades behind us.

Can you handle unit-site and multi-dwelling drainage?

Yes. Unit developments and multi-dwelling sites are core work for us, from the setout through to connection, with the compliance and documentation sorted along the way.

What are aggie drains?

Aggie, or agricultural, drains are subsoil drains that collect and carry away groundwater to keep a site or structure dry. We install site and subsoil drainage as part of the civil works where a site needs it.

Do you do the excavation as well as the drainage?

Yes. Excavation, non-destructive digging, drainage and cut and seal all come under the one contact, so the underground stage is handled end to end rather than split across trades.
